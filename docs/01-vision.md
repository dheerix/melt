## Purpose

Define the direction for MELT as a trusted, founder-operated home bakery serving celebrations in Waterloo Region.

## Current decisions

- Remain purely eggless, small-batch, made-to-order, and preorder-led.
- Build around balanced sweetness, thoughtful presentation, and limited weekly capacity.
- Use Instagram for enquiries while the ordering system is validated.

## Open questions

- What weekly order mix feels sustainable?
- Which occasions and products create the strongest repeat demand?

## Action items

- Write a one-sentence customer promise and review it quarterly.
- Record capacity limits privately; publish no guaranteed availability.

## Metrics

Track enquiry-to-order conversion, repeat-order rate, referral share, and fulfilled slots in private records.
