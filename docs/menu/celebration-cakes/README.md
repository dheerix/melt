# Celebration Cakes

## Category idea

**Familiar celebrations, with a little MELT in them.**

Eggless. Just enough sweet. Made around the person, not around excessive decoration.

The Celebration MELT menu is being developed as a decision system rather than a long flavour list: each cake should give the customer a distinct reason to choose it.

## Current product concepts

### Chocolate — The Everyone Cake

**Status:** CORE — advertise / always available

**Customer:** Kids, adults, families, office celebrations  
**Moment:** Birthdays, anniversaries, congratulations, gatherings  
**Role:** Dependable bestseller and gateway product

**MELT character:** Chocolate-forward, moist, eggless, and not buried under sweetness. Rich because of the chocolate, not because of excessive decoration.

**Formats:** Bento, 1/2 kg, 1 kg+, custom

**Menu copy:**

> Deep chocolate. Soft layers. Just enough sweet.  
> The cake everyone somehow agrees on.

**Decision cue:** *Everyone agrees.*

### Vanilla — The Canvas

### Butterscotch — The Nostalgia Cake

**Status:** CONCEPT / FUTURE CANDIDATE — **do not advertise or list on the customer-facing menu at this time.** Butterscotch is not part of the current MELT menu; this concept is retained for possible future testing/addition.

**Potential customer:** Families, adults with nostalgic taste, mixed-age gatherings, kids  
**Potential moment:** Birthdays, family celebrations, casual anniversaries  
**Potential role:** Familiar crowd-pleaser built around nostalgia, caramel and crunch

**Concept direction:** Soft cake, caramel/butterscotch warmth, light cream and a distinct crunchy component. Keep sweetness restrained; test whether the crunch survives refrigeration and the expected pickup/eating window.

**Potential formats:** 1/2 kg and 1 kg+ first; Bento only if operationally worthwhile

**Draft menu copy (for future use only):**

> Soft layers, caramel warmth and that little crunch you remember.  
> Some birthday flavours never really leave.

**Decision cue:** *I remember this.*

## Desi Celebrations

### Rasmalai — The Desi Celebration

**Status:** SIGNATURE — actively advertise

**Customer:** South Asian families first, while remaining approachable to customers exploring Indian flavours  
**Moment:** Birthdays, anniversaries, housewarmings, family gatherings and festive occasions  
**Role:** MELT signature candidate and brand anchor

**MELT character:** Indian mithai meets celebration cake, with enough restraint that the whole slice remains enjoyable rather than overwhelmingly sweet. Exact construction and ingredient claims depend on the final recipe.

**Formats:** Bento (test/priority), 1/2 kg (hero), 1 kg+, festive editions

**Menu copy:**

> A celebration cake with its heart in mithai.  
> Familiar, festive and just enough sweet.

**Decision cue:** *Desi, but balanced.*

**Brand role:** Chocolate can be a sales anchor; Rasmalai can be a brand anchor.

### Gulab Jamun — The Warm Celebration

**Status:** CORE DESI — advertise

**Customer:** South Asian families, mithai lovers and festive hosts  
**Moment:** Birthdays, anniversaries, housewarmings, family gatherings and festivals  
**Role:** Joyful, mithai-forward alternative to Rasmalai

**MELT character:** Warm, indulgent and unmistakably celebratory. Product development should specifically manage sweetness stacking across cake, cream, syrup and gulab jamun.

**Formats:** 1/2 kg (hero), 1 kg+; Bento only after testing

**Menu copy:**

> Cake met mithai at the celebration table.  
> Familiar, indulgent — and still just enough sweet.

**Decision cue:** *Joyful Desi.*

**Internal distinction:** Rasmalai = elegant/milky/aromatic. Gulab Jamun = warm/joyful/mithai-forward.

## MELT Specials

### Kunafa — The Crunchy One

**Status:** MELT SPECIAL — advertise, but do not call it a permanent signature yet

**Customer:** Younger adults, adventurous dessert buyers and customers looking for something different  
**Moment:** Birthdays, dinner parties, gifting and discovery purchases  
**Role:** Texture/discovery product

**MELT character:** The reason for Kunafa to exist is texture: soft cake, creamy elements and a distinct crisp/crunchy component. Product testing must validate texture after assembly, refrigeration, transport and holding.

**Formats:** 1/2 kg (hero), 1 kg+; Bento experimental initially

**Menu copy:**

> Creamy, crunchy and made for trying something different.

**Decision cue:** *Something different — with crunch.*

**Positioning rule:** Do not build the permanent identity around the Dubai-chocolate trend. Kunafa should earn its place through taste, texture retention and repeat demand.

## Product-development rules

1. **A concept is not automatically an active menu item.** R&D ideas can remain documented without appearing publicly.
2. **Do not make unverified ingredient claims.** Terms such as fresh fruit, compote, cream cheese, specific nuts or preparations belong in customer copy only after recipes are locked.
3. **Not every flavour needs to be a signature.** MELT needs sales anchors, brand anchors, supporting core products, specials and seasonal products.
4. **Do not force every flavour into Bento.** Scale down only when flavour, texture and operations still work.
5. **Just Enough Sweet is a product constraint.** Especially for mithai-based cakes, sweetness should be balanced across the whole slice rather than judged component-by-component.
6. **Decoration supports the cake.** Avoid making visual excess the default definition of premium.
