## Purpose

Support traceability and clear allergy conversations without making unsupported safety claims.

## Current decisions

- State that products are made without eggs; do not represent the kitchen as allergen-free.
- Keep brands, lot details, labels, substitutions, and customer allergy disclosures in protected records.

## Open questions

- Which official record-retention and labelling rules apply?
- How are supplier formulation changes detected?

## Action items

- Photograph labels and record lot/date information privately.
- Re-check every substitution and communicate uncertainty before confirmation.

## Metrics

Track substitution checks, missing records, disclosed allergies, and near misses privately.
