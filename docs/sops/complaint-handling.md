## Purpose

Respond promptly, fairly, and safely while learning from each issue.

## Current decisions

- Acknowledge, gather facts, assess safety urgency, propose a proportionate resolution, and record corrective action.
- Keep identities, order details, photos, refunds, and operational incidents out of Git.

## Open questions

- Which events require notification, product withdrawal, or expert advice?
- What resolution authority and response target are sustainable?

## Action items

- Create a private incident form and escalation contact list.
- Verify reporting duties with official authorities.

## Metrics

Track response time, issue type, resolution time, repeat causes, and corrective-action closure privately.
