## Purpose

Encourage trusted local word of mouth in a way that remains personal and manageable.

## Current decisions

- Ask satisfied customers to share MELT naturally after successful handoff.
- Do not publish names, messages, or photos without explicit permission.

## Open questions

- Is a simple thank-you enough, or would a structured benefit be useful?
- How will referrals be attributed without collecting unnecessary data?

## Action items

- Draft a post-order thank-you message.
- Test one lightweight referral prompt before adding incentives.

## Metrics

Track referral share, referred-customer conversion, repeat orders, and administrative effort privately.
