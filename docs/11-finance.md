## Purpose

Protect sustainable decisions through accurate, private unit economics and cash records.

## Current decisions

- Include ingredients, packaging, labour time, fees, waste, and overhead in product decisions.
- Never commit purchase costs, pricing calculations, revenue, profit, tax IDs, or account details to this repository.

## Open questions

- What target contribution supports sustainable production?

## Action items

## Metrics

Track food cost percentage, packaging cost, order margin, waste cost, cash collection, and forecast variance privately.
