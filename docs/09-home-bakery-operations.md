## Purpose

Coordinate preorder intake, production capacity, ingredient inventory, and pickup from a founder-operated home bakery.

## Current decisions

- Use weekly slots and production limits rather than walk-in hours.
- Share pickup details only after confirmation; never publish a residential address.
- Treat Ontario and Waterloo requirements as a checklist until verified with official authorities.

## Open questions

- What is the safe weekly capacity by product mix?
- Which permits, inspections, labelling, tax, zoning, and insurance requirements apply?

## Action items

- Verify current requirements with official municipal, regional, provincial, and federal sources.
- Maintain private inventory, traceability, pickup, and incident records.

## Metrics

Track capacity utilization, late handoffs, waste, stockouts, and schedule variance privately.
