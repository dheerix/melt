## Purpose

Plan a safe, achievable production week around confirmed preorders.

## Current decisions

- Schedule backward from handoff, grouping compatible work without exceeding storage or equipment limits.
- Keep customer details, recipes, quantities, and exact schedules in private records.

## Open questions

- What buffer is needed by product and complexity?
- What is the maximum safe concurrent order mix?

## Action items

- Create private weekly production and procurement sheets.
- Include cooling, decorating, packing, cleaning, and contingency time.

## Metrics

Track plan variance, overtime, missed checks, and unused capacity privately.
