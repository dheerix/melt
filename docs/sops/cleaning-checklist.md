## Purpose

Provide a reusable framework for safe preparation and close-down cleaning.

## Current decisions

- Cover hands and attire, food-contact surfaces, tools, equipment, cloths, waste, storage, and pest observations.
- Follow verified product directions and applicable food-safety requirements.

## Open questions

- Which sanitizer, concentration, contact time, and test method are approved for this setting?
- What verification records are required locally?

## Action items

- Confirm the checklist with current official guidance.
- Store completed logs and incident details privately.

## Metrics

Track checklist completion, verification failures, and corrective actions privately.
