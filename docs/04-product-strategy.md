## Purpose

Shape a focused collection that fits MELT’s promise and home-bakery capacity.

## Current decisions

- Lead with celebration cakes and feature rasmalai-inspired flavour direction.
- Support with cupcakes, dessert boxes, and selective seasonal specials.
- Avoid public prices until portions, labour, and costs are validated.

## Open questions

- Which products travel and hold quality best?
- Which variations add customer value without creating operational drag?

## Action items

- Define private product scorecards for quality, demand, effort, and margin.
- Retire or revise products that repeatedly strain production.

## Metrics

Track product mix, acceptance rate, production time, quality issues, and repeat demand privately.
