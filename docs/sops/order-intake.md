## Purpose

Turn Instagram enquiries into complete, confirmed orders without storing customer details in Git.

## Current decisions

- Collect date, occasion, servings, flavour direction, design references, allergy disclosure, pickup needs, and preferred contact path in a private order record.
- An enquiry is not confirmed until scope, price, payment terms, and pickup expectations are accepted.

## Open questions

- Which responses can be templated while remaining personal?
- What response-time commitment is sustainable?

## Action items

- Create a private intake form and confirmation template.
- Record consent before using order photos or feedback.

## Metrics

Track response time, incomplete enquiries, conversion, and reasons orders are declined.
