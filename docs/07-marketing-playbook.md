## Purpose

Create repeatable, local demand without exceeding weekly production capacity.

## Current decisions

- Focus on Instagram, local referrals, seasonal moments, and repeat customers.
- Market available preorder windows, not storefront hours or walk-in specials.

## Open questions

- Which local partnerships fit the brand and compliance needs?
- How should waitlist demand be handled?

## Action items

- Plan campaigns backward from order deadlines and capacity.
- Record channel attribution privately.

## Metrics

Track qualified enquiries, conversion by source, capacity fill, repeat rate, and campaign effort.
