## Purpose

Keep MELT distinct, recognizable, and consistent.

## Current decisions

- Position around purposeful eggless baking—not substitution.
- Use “Purely eggless. Just enough sweet.” and a quiet-luxury visual language.
- Sound warm, concise, confident, and never overclaim.

## Open questions

- Which customer words best describe the desired experience?
- Which messages lead to qualified celebration enquiries?

## Action items

- Maintain a public-safe message library.
- Audit captions and packaging each quarter for consistency.

## Metrics

Track saves, shares, profile visits, qualified enquiries, and unaided phrases customers repeat.
