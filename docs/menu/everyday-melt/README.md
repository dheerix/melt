# Everyday MELT

## Category concept

**Cake doesn't need an occasion.**

Everyday MELT is the repeat-purchase engine: unfrosted or simply finished cakes for chai, coffee, lunchboxes, guests and ordinary days.

Working category copy:

> **Everyday MELT**  
> No frosting. No occasion. No excuse needed.  
> Eggless bakes made for chai, coffee and everything in between.  
> *Just enough sweet.*

Visual direction: warm, homemade and premium rather than celebration-style decoration.

## Product concepts

### Walnut Cake — The Chai Cake
- **Customer:** adults, families, parents, hosts
- **Moment:** morning coffee, evening chai, guests
- **Character:** soft cake with generous walnuts; restrained sweetness
- **Format:** loaf; mini loaf may be tested later
- **Behaviour:** strong repeat potential
- **Copy:** “Soft, nutty and uncomplicated. The kind of cake that makes you put the kettle on.”
- **Visual:** sliced loaf, visible walnut pieces, chai/coffee nearby; minimal decoration

### Dry Fruit Cake — The Pantry Cake
- **Customer:** families, older customers, gifting
- **Moment:** chai, visiting someone's home, holidays
- **Character:** richer and more substantial than Walnut; fruits and nuts
- **Format:** loaf / gift loaf
- **Behaviour:** repeat + gift
- **Copy:** “Fruits, nuts and a little patience in every slice.”
- **Seasonal extension:** Christmas/holiday edition

### Marble Cake — The Childhood One
- **Customer:** families and kids
- **Moment:** lunchbox, after school, coffee
- **Character:** chocolate + vanilla nostalgia
- **Format:** loaf
- **Behaviour:** repeat
- **Copy:** “Chocolate couldn't decide. Vanilla wouldn't either. So we kept both.”

### Orange Cake — The Bright One
- **Customer:** adults; coffee/tea audience
- **Moment:** brunch, afternoon tea, spring/summer hosting
- **Character:** fresh and bright among warmer/nuttier flavours
- **Copy:** “Soft, citrusy and bright — a little sunshine with your tea.”
- **Note:** ingredient claims such as real zest/juice should only be used if they match the final recipe.

### Banana Cake — The Comfort Cake
- **Customer:** families
- **Moment:** everyday comfort/snacking
- **Decision needed:** define whether the final product is cake-like or banana-bread-like.
- **Copy:** “Familiar banana comfort, baked a little softer.”
- **Possible variant:** Walnut Banana rather than a separate large SKU family.

### Everyday Chocolate
Distinct from the decorated Chocolate Celebration Cake.
- **Customer:** broad appeal, especially families/children
- **Moment:** school snack, evening craving, coffee
- **Format:** loaf / mini loaf
- **Behaviour:** repeat
- **Copy:** “Because chocolate doesn't need a birthday.”

### Dates & Walnut — Signature Candidate
- **Customer:** adults, families, chai/coffee drinkers, gifting
- **Moment:** tea, hosting, everyday treat
- **Format:** loaf / gift loaf
- **Behaviour:** strong repeat
- **Positioning:** naturally aligned with “just enough sweet” without health claims
- **Copy:** “Rich dates. Toasty walnuts. Just enough sweet.”

### Rava Cake — Indian-home Connection
- **Customer:** Indian families initially; approachable to a wider audience
- **Moment:** chai, breakfast-ish snack, visiting family
- **Character:** simple, nostalgic, home-style
- **Copy:** “A familiar Indian bake, made for slow cups of chai.”
- **Brand role:** Indian × Canadian character without forcing a “fusion” label.

### Plum Cake — Seasonal
Do not rely on it as a year-round hero initially.
- **Campaign:** **MELT Holiday Plum Cake**
- **Window:** November–December
- **Opportunity:** pre-orders, gift sizes, corporate/gifting boxes

### Ghee Cake — Signature Candidate
- **Character:** rich, aromatic, nostalgic
- **Pairing:** masala chai
- **Visual:** simple; flavour/aroma is the story
- **Copy:** “Rich, aromatic and quietly nostalgic.”

### Atta & Jaggery — Signature Candidate
Potential identity product.
- Avoid “healthy,” “diet,” and “guilt-free” positioning.
- Lean on ingredient simplicity and MELT's restrained-sweetness philosophy.
- **Copy:** “Whole wheat. Jaggery. Nothing trying too hard.”

### Ragi Chocolate Banana — Signature Candidate
- **Character:** traditional grain + familiar chocolate/banana comfort
- **Role:** distinctive enough to stop the scroll
- Avoid medicinal/health-product positioning.
- **Copy:** “Ragi met chocolate. Banana made them get along.”

### Tutti Frutti — Nostalgia Cake
- **Character:** Indian childhood bakery nostalgia
- **Copy:** “Some flavours don't grow up. Thankfully.”
- **Content opportunity:** nostalgia-led storytelling.

### Choco-Chip Loaf
- **Customer:** families/kids; mainstream repeat product
- **Role:** reliable rather than flagship
- **Copy:** “Soft cake. Chocolate pockets. Gone surprisingly quickly.”

### Mango Cake — Seasonal
Place under a coordinated **Mango Season at MELT** campaign rather than treating each mango product independently.
Potential campaign family:
- Mango Loaf
- Mango Celebration Cake
- Mango Bento
- Mango Cheesecake, if product testing supports it
