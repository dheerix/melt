## Purpose

Create consistent, honest imagery that lets MELT’s bakes provide most of the colour.

## Current decisions

- Use soft natural side light, warm neutral backgrounds, deep-green accents, restrained brass, and real product texture.
- Avoid heavy filters, clutter, misleading scale, and images of products MELT cannot reproduce.

| Use | Direction | Aspect ratio | Suggested export |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Hero | Whole signature cake; negative space for copy | 16:9 | 1920×1080 WebP |
| Product cards | Consistent three-quarter or overhead angle | 5:4 | 1200×960 WebP |
| Cake details | Finish, crumb, piping, garnish | 4:5 | 1200×1500 WebP |
| Packaging | Closed and opened presentation | 4:5 | 1200×1500 WebP |
| Founder portrait | Garima in a natural making context | 4:5 | 1600×2000 WebP |
